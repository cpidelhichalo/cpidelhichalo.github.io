About the CPI Delhi Chalo Campaign

The Communist Party of India is preparing for a powerful and historic “Delhi Chalo” national rally on September 1, 2026, at Ramlila Maidan in New Delhi. Under the stirring slogan “Badlaav Zaroori Hai” (Change is Necessary), the campaign will unite workers, peasants, students, and women in one urgent call for justice, dignity, and democratic rights.

  • Price Rise Must End
  • Jobs For Every Youth
  • Universal Public Health & Education
  • Immediate Implementation of Women's Reservation
  • Legal Guarantee For MSP
  • Defend Workers' Rights
  • Restore Ballot Paper, Save Democracy
  • Justice For Dalits And Adivasis: End Atrocities And Displacement

The CPI is also mobilizing participation from far-off regions, including Kerala, through a special 18-coach train carrying over 1,500 party workers, reflecting the scale and determination of this campaign.CPI's Kerala unit chartered an entire train to take party workers to New Delhi for the "Delhi Chalo" protest against the Union government
